How to play

Back to the garden

The loop

  1. Read the hamster. Each 10-minute round ends as a nap, a jog or zoomies. The bars on the play screen show the last 24.
  2. Plant for his next mood. The colored band on a packet says what feeds it. Pick a packet, then tap a plot. Seeds react from the next round.
  3. Pick what ripens. Tap a ripe plant within 12 rounds or it rots. Plants keep growing while the tab is closed.
  4. Grow the garden. Pips buy seeds, tools and plots. Points raise your level, which unlocks rarer seeds.

A real round takes 10 minutes. Switch to Demo on the play screen to see a full cycle in about a minute.

Good to know

  • Mixed beds pay more. A plant picked with two or more other species next to it pays +20%.
  • Watering can. Gives a growing plant one step of help, then refills after 6 rounds.
  • Glass cloche. Saves a plant from the next thing that would wilt it.
  • Daily challenges. Three new ones arrive at 00:00 UTC, and each pays pips once.
  • Combos. Pick different plants within 3 rounds of each other for extra pips: 5%, then 10%, then up to 15%. Points and XP stay the same.
  • Seasons. Scores reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Quests change daily.
  • Without a wallet. Your garden is saved in this browser only, and it stays there if you connect later.
  • With a wallet. You sign a message, which costs no gas and sends no transaction. You get a new garden kept by the server, so it follows you across devices and its score counts on the Live board.
  • Practice. Demo rounds are simulated. Demo scores have their own board and never count towards Live.
